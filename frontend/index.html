Welcome to Dam Sen Park

Dam Sen Cultural Park features 13 thrill rides (Roller Coaster, Waterfall Flume, Power Surge, and more), 5 modern virtual interactive attractions, 5 relaxing rides, 12 children's rides, and many other exciting games.

Dam Sen is also known as a zoo capable of breeding rare and endangered species listed in the Red Book, such as Sumatran orangutans (which have successfully bred twice), yellow-cheeked gibbons, adjutant storks, and painted storks. In addition, the park features an Aquarium home to a rich variety of marine life and Amazonian fish, including sharks and 2-meter arapaima.
